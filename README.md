## 📌 Overview

The **Structural Delineation Framework (SDF)** addresses the fundamental structural fragmentation in modern theoretical physics (e.g., the disconnect between quantum mechanics, general relativity, and non-equilibrium statistical mechanics). 

Instead of freezing descriptions within isolated, intermediate **carrier manifolds** (such as rigid Hilbert spaces or continuous Riemannian metrics), SDF establishes a mathematically rigorous, multi-tiered delineation sequence starting from an **Unconstrained Ground ($G$)** to **Manifest Physical Form ($F$)**.

---

## 🧬 Canonical Sequence ($G \to \text{Cid} \to S \to R \to M \to L \to F$)

SDF enforces a strictly ordered, "no-merger" progression that guarantees causal closure, scale-invariant stability, and projective consistency:

1. **$G$ (Unconstrained Ground / Bonyad-e-La-Gheyd):** Pre-geometric, unconditioned ground state devoid of prior metric or topological constraints.
2. **$\text{Cid}$ (Topological Delineation):** Primary boundary demarcation and non-trivial invariant selection.
3. **$S$ (State Space Structuring):** Stratification of relational states and metric emergence.
4. **$R$ (Relational Dynamics & Projection):** Multi-tier carrier dynamics and projective reductions.
5. **$M$ (Manifold Mapping & Information Geometry):** Metric-affine connections and information-geometric manifold formalization.
6. **$L$ (Lattice Formalism):** Discretization, kinetic stability closures, and quantum lattice regularizations.
7. **$F$ (Manifest Form):** Observable asymptotic physical dynamics and field configurations.
