## 📌 Overview

The **Structural Delineation Framework (SDF)** addresses the fundamental structural fragmentation in modern theoretical physics (e.g., the disconnect between quantum mechanics, general relativity, and non-equilibrium statistical mechanics). 

Instead of freezing descriptions within isolated, intermediate **carrier manifolds** (such as rigid Hilbert spaces or continuous Riemannian metrics), SDF establishes a mathematically rigorous, multi-tiered delineation sequence starting from an **Unconstrained Ground ($G$)** to **Manifest Physical Form ($F$)**.
